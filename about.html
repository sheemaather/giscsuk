A Legacy of Excellence

The college has evolved through several key phases to become the premier institution it is today: • Foundation (1956): Established to meet the growing demand for high-quality education in Sindh. • Expansion (1962): The college transitioned to its current, expansive purpose-built campus to accommodate a growing student body and modernized facilities. • Nationalization (1972): In September 1972, the institution was brought under state management, further solidifying its role as a cornerstone of public sector education. • Bifurcation & Specialization (1973): To optimize academic focus, the institution was bifurcated into two independent colleges operating within the same historic premises. Government Islamia Science College was designated for the Morning Shift, while Government Islamia Arts & Commerce College serves the Evening Shift.
