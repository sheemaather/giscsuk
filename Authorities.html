PROF. SHAFIQUE AHMED RIND

Regional Director Colleges Sukkur District

Shafeeque Ahmed Rind is a government education administrator associated with the College Education Department of Sindh. His reported service in the regional colleges administration includes serving as Regional Director of Colleges in the Larkana Region in 2017. The Regional Directorate system of the Directorate General Colleges Sindh is responsible for administrative coordination and oversight of government colleges within its respective region. The Sukkur Regional Directorate operates within the administrative structure of the Directorate General Colleges Sindh and provides coordination and oversight for government colleges in the Sukkur region. The official Sindh Colleges portal lists government colleges in Sukkur District, including Government Islamia Science College Sukkur, Government Degree Boys College Sukkur, Government Girls Degree College Sukkur and other institutions.

PROF. SHAHID AHMED KHAN

Principal Government Islamia Science College Sukkur

Professor of English (BS-20), Prof. Shahid Ahmed Khan is an experienced educator and faculty member associated with Government Islamia Science College Sukkur. He has served in the field of higher education with a particular academic focus on English language and literature. According to an official Government of Sindh College Education Department notification, Prof. Shahid Ahmed Khan was serving as Associate Professor of English (BS-19) at Government Islamia Science College Sukkur. The notification also records his M.Phil degree in English from Shah Abdul Latif University, Khairpur. With his academic background and teaching experience, Prof. Shahid Ahmed Khan has contributed to the academic environment of Government Islamia Science College Sukkur, supporting students in the study of English and contributing to the institution's broader educational mission. Government Islamia Science College Sukkur is one of the established public-sector colleges in Sukkur and offers programmes in Science and Computer Science according to the Government of Sindh's official colleges listing.
