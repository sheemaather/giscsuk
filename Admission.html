Admission Criteria & Information

Government Islamia Science College Sukkur offers pathways from Higher Secondary education through Associate and undergraduate degree programs. The information below provides a clear website structure for applicants; final eligibility, merit percentages, deadlines, fees and seat availability should always follow the latest official admission notice.

ELIGIBILITY

Admission Criteria by Program

Program 01

HSC-I

Higher Secondary Science admission for students entering XI.

  • Candidates must have successfully completed the SSC-II (Science Group) examination.
  • A minimum of "C" Grade is required for consideration.
  • Applicants from the BISE, Sukkur, are eligible directly.
  • Original academic documents must be produced at verification.
  • Candidates with equivalent qualifications from other educational boards must obtain an Eligibility Certificate from BISE, Sukkur, prior to admission.
Program 02

Associate Degree (ADS Part-I)

A two-year undergraduate pathway for students who have completed Higher Secondary education.

  • Completion of HSC-II (Pre-Engineering or Pre-Medical).
  • A minimum of 45% aggregate marks is required.
  • Candidates with equivalent qualifications from other educational boards must obtain an Eligibility Certificate from BISE, Sukkur, prior to admission.
  • Students must satisfy any affiliation or university requirements.
Program 03

BS Computer Science

A four-year undergraduate computing program for students seeking careers in modern technology.

  • HSSC / Intermediate / FSc or equivalent 12-year qualification.
  • A minimum of 45% aggregate marks is mandatory for enrollment in the semester system.
  • e, entry test and merit formula follow the admitting institution's policy.
  • Foreign qualifications may require an equivalence certificate.
  • Fee Structure at the time of admission
Program 04

BS Zoology

A four-year science degree focusing on animal diversity, physiology, ecology, genetics and research.

  • HSSC / Intermediate or equivalent 12-year qualification.
  • Science group with Biology / General Biology as required.
  • Minimum percentage and admission test requirements may be set by the admitting institution.
  • Selection is based on the approved merit and admission policy.
Website administration note: Do not hard-code a permanent minimum percentage unless it has been approved in the current admission advertisement. Update this section every admission cycle.
HOW TO APPLY

Admission Process

FSc Admission

1

Check the Advertisement

Review group eligibility, seats, dates, documents and current merit requirements.

2

Submit Application

Complete the prescribed college/online application and attach required documents.

3

Merit & Verification

Applications are checked and shortlisted according to the applicable merit policy.

4

Confirm Admission

Selected students complete verification and fee/enrolment requirements.

ADS Admission

1

Check Eligibility

Confirm HSC/equivalent qualification and subject requirements.

2

Apply & Submit Documents

Submit the application, academic record and documents requested.

3

Merit / Selection

Applications are processed according to approved merit and seat allocation.

4

Enrolment

Complete verification, fee payment and registration before the deadline.

BSCS & BS Zoology

1

Check Program Eligibility

Confirm HSSC subject combination and minimum marks for the current cycle.

2

Submit Application

Complete the prescribed application and submit academic and identification documents.

3

Test / Merit / Interview

Where required, applicants appear in the prescribed test or interview and are ranked under the approved formula.

4

Final Enrolment

Selected candidates complete verification, fee payment and enrolment formalities.
