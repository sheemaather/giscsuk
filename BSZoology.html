BS ZOOLOGY PROGRAM

BACHELOR OF SCIENCE — ZOOLOGY

BS Zoology at Government Islamia Science College Sukkur

The BS Zoology program is a four-year degree offering in-depth study of animal biology, physiology, genetics and ecology, combined with hands-on laboratory and field work. Graduates are prepared for careers in research, wildlife conservation, healthcare-adjacent fields and further postgraduate study.

AREAS OF FOCUS

Specializations

Study of animal habitats, biodiversity and conservation practices for endangered species.

  • Wildlife Biology

  • Marine Biology

Genetics & Physiology
