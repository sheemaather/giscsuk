HIGHER SECONDARY EDUCATION

HSC at Government Islamia Science College Sukkur

Our HSC programs provide students with a strong foundation in science, analytical thinking, practical laboratory work and preparation for university-level study. Students can choose from Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering and Computer Science according to their interests and future career goals.
