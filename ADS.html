ADS PROGRAM

ASSOCIATE DEGREE IN SCIENCE

ADS at Government Islamia Science College Sukkur

The Associate Degree in Science (ADS) is a two-year undergraduate program equivalent to the first two years of a BS degree. It gives students a strong grounding in core science subjects and a direct pathway into a relevant BS program through credit transfer, without needing to repeat coursework.
